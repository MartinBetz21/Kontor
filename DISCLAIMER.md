## For your own books only

Kontor is software for keeping the books of your own business. It is not tax advice, legal advice or accounting advice, and it does not replace a tax advisor, accountant or lawyer. Nobody who publishes or develops Kontor gives you advice on your individual case, and using Kontor does not create a client or service relationship with anyone.

If you keep books, prepare VAT returns or file taxes for other people, you need the qualification and registration your country's law requires. In Germany that is regulated by the Steuerberatungsgesetz (StBerG, in particular §§ 2, 3, 5 and 6). Kontor does not change who may do that work.

## You stay responsible

You alone are responsible for your bookkeeping and your filings: that the entries are correct and complete, that you keep records as long as the law requires (in Germany the GoBD and the retention periods of § 147 AO), that you meet deadlines, and that what you send to a tax authority is right. Check every figure, every booking and every filing yourself before you rely on it.

Whether Kontor fits your situation is your decision. It covers German rules for small businesses and limited companies as one user set them up at one point in time. Tax law, forms and data formats change every year, so parts of Kontor may be outdated, incomplete or wrong for you. Situations Kontor does not support are listed in [ONBOARDING.md](ONBOARDING.md).

## Not certified

Kontor is not certified or audited accounting software. It has no IDW PS 880 attestation and no GoBD certificate. Its tests check that the code does what the code intends, not that it meets every legal requirement for your business.

## ELSTER

Kontor does not include the ELSTER ERiC library, no manufacturer ID (Hersteller-ID) and no certificate. If you want to validate or send filings electronically, you obtain ERiC from the official ELSTER developer area under its own licence, register for your own Hersteller-ID, and use your own certificate. Never put a certificate, PIN or Hersteller-ID into this repository.
